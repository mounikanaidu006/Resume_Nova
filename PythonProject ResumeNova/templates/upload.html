Evidence-Based Analysis

ResumeNova does not assign scores based on a predefined job role. The analysis is generated from the uploaded resume, its detectable PDF structure, the role entered, and the supplied job description.

A requirement shown as “not found” means that ResumeNova could not detect that requirement in the resume. It does not automatically mean that the candidate does not possess that skill.
